# Civic and transport pack — provenance

Created for City on 2026-09-11. Scope: all 15 assets listed in [civic_transport_manifest.json](../civic_transport_manifest.json), including their baked `*_diffuse.png` files.

All model geometry and bitmap sign lettering were authored for this repository. No third-party vehicle/building model, manufacturer's logo, font file, or architectural drawing was imported. Geometry follows the repository owner's distribution policy; this record does not assign a new license to the repository.

The materials reuse these Poly Haven photographs by **Rob Tuytel**, licensed **CC0 1.0**:

- [Grey Plaster 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/grey_plaster_02)
- [Wood Planks Grey](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks_grey)
- [Brick Wall 11](https://polyhaven.com/a/brick_wall_11)
- [Concrete](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete)

No additional texture downloads were needed for this pack. Original texture files remain in `App/assets/third_party/polyhaven/`. The first three download URLs, dates, and SHA-256 values are recorded in [REALISTIC_MATERIALS.json](REALISTIC_MATERIALS.json) and each source directory's `SOURCE.json`. The pre-existing concrete source is recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md](THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md).

License references: [Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license), [CC0 1.0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode). Photographs were tiled, tinted and combined with original material colors and local ambient occlusion, then baked into single-material textures. No direct scene lighting was baked. The editable Blender file packs these derivative textures. Blender/Python executables are not distributed with the assets.
